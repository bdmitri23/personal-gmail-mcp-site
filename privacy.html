Privacy policy

Personal Gmail MCP. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner of this app, on the owner's own Gmail account. There are no other users, and the app is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Messages, labels, filters and drafts in the owner's own Gmail account, through Google's Gmail API, with two permissions: gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic. It has no permission to delete mail permanently, and it is built without any way to send or trash mail.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own requests: searching, labelling, archiving, creating filters and saving drafts. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling, and nothing is sold.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the owner's computer and talks only to Google's Gmail API. Mail content the owner asks the assistant to read is processed by the owner's AI assistant provider as part of the owner's own session. Nothing is shared with anyone else. Working files stay on the owner's computer and are deleted when a task ends.

Revoking access

The Google authorization is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
